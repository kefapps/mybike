## 3. MYB-137 Calibration Verdict

Premium target reached:

- No

Closure verdict:

- Checkpoint insuffisant

Score band:

- Prototype-level / weak checkpoint

previous baseline, but the after capture still shows prototype-level weaknesses:
pole-like trunks, weak canopy, sparse forest floor, flat lighting/fog, rough
material language, and little composition rhythm.

The result is useful evidence. It is not final visual quality.

## 5. Before / After Delta

Purpose:

This section explains visible progression from the previous baseline to
MYB-137 after. It is qualitative evidence only. The before state is not fully
scored with the 9-criterion rubric.

### What Improved

- The after route capture is more enveloping than the before capture.
- There is more vertical rhythm along the side corridor.
- The route feels less isolated from the surrounding forest bands.
- Midground density improved enough to confirm that vertical volume is a useful
  direction.

### What Remains Insufficient

- The new vertical elements still read mostly as simple trunks/posts.
- The forest floor does not yet carry moss, leaves, earth, stones, and grounded
  transitions in a premium way.
- Lighting and fog do not yet create an enchanted, scenic, premium mood.
- Canopy and background depth remain underdeveloped.
- The scene has little memorable rhythm or landmark structure.

### Why The Verdict Remains Checkpoint Insuffisant

- Relative improvement does not prove `Premium target`.
- All blocking criteria remain below 4.
- The capture still reads as a useful prototype proof rather than production
  Art Rescue quality.
- Future tickets must validate improvements from the canonical route camera, not
  from this diagnostic baseline alone.

## 8. Future Ticket Review Rule

Future Art Rescue visual tickets must not use MYB-137 as final quality proof.

Required final evidence for visible production tickets:

- canonical route-camera screenshot;
- overview screenshot from the same scene state;
- filled 9-criterion rubric;
- visual verdict;
- human validation when the judgment is subjective;
- relevant validators or documented warnings when assets, scene, materials,
  lighting, density, collisions, or performance are changed.

MYB-137 can remain a baseline, counter-example, and calibration reference.

It cannot be used as:

- `Premium target` proof;
- asset promotion proof;
- final production validation;
- closure proof for future visual tickets.
