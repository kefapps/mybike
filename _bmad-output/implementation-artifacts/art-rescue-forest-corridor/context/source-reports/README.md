## Context

This is a Unity first-person cycling game scene. The target is a stylized premium forest corridor around a ride route.

We moved away from isolated forest ground ribbons and toward a route-first corridor strategy. The corridor structure now works better, but the image still feels poor, flat and prototype-like.

MYB-137 specifically tests vertical volume: closer trunks, leaning trunks and rising root clusters around the route edge.

## Known Problems

- The route is more enveloped after MYB-137, but some trunks read as simple posts.
- Ground material is still weak.
- Lighting/fog is flat and grey.
- Asset variety is insufficient.
- Strong memorable silhouettes are still missing.
- The scene still looks like a preview/prototype, not final production art.

## Question For ChatGPT Pro

Using the images and reports in this zip, diagnose why the forest corridor still looks poor and propose a concrete art direction / production plan:

- what is visually wrong;
- what asset families are missing;
- how to improve density, variation, verticality, material, lighting and strong silhouettes;
- what order to do the work in;
