## What Changed From V2

V3 keeps the V2 corridor structure and adds a first ground-matter pass:

- smaller leaf-litter patches;
- moss patches on the outer verge;
- exposed soil scars near one shoulder;
- 40 simple vertical understory tufts;
- no large rectangular canopy-shadow bands after visual review showed they looked artificial.

## Visual Verdict

V3 is better than V2 because the corridor now has readable low-height volume and some surface hierarchy from the ride camera.

It is still not final art. The understory tufts are too symbolic and triangular, and the material variation is still primitive. However, the pass confirms the correct next direction: forest quality should come from authored low ground volumes, root clusters, moss/leaf shapes and trunk silhouettes, not from flat surface ribbons alone.

## Recommendation

Use V3 as the current MYB-114 direction checkpoint.

Next production work should replace the placeholder tufts with authored repeatable forest-floor modules: fern clusters, leaf piles, moss mats, small broken branches, exposed roots and richer trunk bases. These should stay route-driven so the corridor keeps its readable ride composition.
