## Visual Verdict

The after image is more enveloping than MYB-114 V4 from the route camera. The road edges now have stronger vertical rhythm and the forest no longer reads only as low ground ribbons.

The result is not final art. Some trunks still read as simple cylinders or posts, especially the closest vertical pieces. This is acceptable for MYB-137 as a volume proof, but the forms need asset-quality silhouettes before final integration.
